FAQ
How do I trigger manual irrigation?
Go to Trees&Zones → Device Control and toggle the Device switch to ON.
What does soil moisture % mean?
Soil moisture % represents the volumetric water content of the soil. For Harumanis mango, the optimal range is 55–70%. Below 45% triggers an alert; below 42% requires immediate irrigation. Above 80% may indicate over-irrigation risk.
How is Grade A / B / C determined?
Grade A: Fruit weight ≥550g, full golden-yellow colour, no blemishes, excellent aroma.
Grade B: 450–549g, minor surface marks, good colour.
Grade C: Under 450g, visible scarring, or colour defects. Grade C is typically sold locally at lower price.
How do I export a report?
Go to the Analytics page and click Export Analytics Report (PDF) or Export CSV Data. For harvest records, go to Harvest & Trace and use the Export PDF or Export CSV buttons at the top of the harvest table.
